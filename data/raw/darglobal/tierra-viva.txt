What is Tierra Viva? Tierra Viva is a limited collection of ultra-luxury gated villas in Benahavís, Spain, developed by DarGlobal with design inspired by Automobili Lamborghini. Villas are staggered across the hillside at different levels in the Los Jaralillos district to ensure every home captures unobstructed panoramas of the Mediterranean Sea.

Tierra Viva is a limited collection of ultra-luxury gated villas in Benahavís, Spain, developed by DarGlobal with design inspired by Automobili Lamborghini. Villas are staggered across the hillside at different levels in the Los Jaralillos district to ensure every home captures unobstructed panoramas of the Mediterranean Sea.

Where exactly is Tierra Viva located? Tierra Viva is in the hills of Los Jaralillos, Benahavís, 15 minutes from Marbella and within easy reach of Puerto Banús and Sotogrande. The location gives direct access to 12 nearby golf clubs, Andalusian villages, and the shores of the Mediterranean, while remaining in one of the most prestigious residential areas on the Costa Del Sol.

What amenities does Tierra Viva offer? Tierra Viva is a gated community with 24/7 premium security, an exclusive residents' clubhouse, sauna, steam room, and private pool areas.
