What is Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai? Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai is the first and only Trump-branded development in the Middle East, delivered by DarGlobal. It combines a five-star hotel, private residences, and a members-only club in a single address on Sheikh Zayed Road, with direct access to Downtown Dubai.

Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai is the first and only Trump-branded development in the Middle East, delivered by DarGlobal. It combines a five-star hotel, private residences, and a members-only club in a single address on Sheikh Zayed Road, with direct access to Downtown Dubai.

What unit types and sizes are available at Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai? The Trump Residences offer one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments and four-bedroom duplex penthouses, ranging from 109 to 722 sqm. Every unit is positioned to deliver a guaranteed view of either the Arabian Sea or the Burj Khalifa.

The Trump Residences offer one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments and four-bedroom duplex penthouses, ranging from 109 to 722 sqm. Every unit is positioned to deliver a guaranteed view of either the Arabian Sea or the Burj Khalifa.

When will Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai be completed? The project is under development with handover scheduled for December 2031. DarGlobal is the master developer. Buyers purchasing off-plan should contact the DarGlobal sales team directly for current payment plan terms and availability.

Where exactly is Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai located? The tower sits on Sheikh Zayed Road at the gateway to Downtown Dubai, giving residents direct access to Dubai Mall, the Dubai Fountains, and the Burj Khalifa district while bypassing the congestion of central Downtown.

The tower sits on Sheikh Zayed Road at the gateway to Downtown Dubai, giving residents direct access to Dubai Mall, the Dubai Fountains, and the Burj Khalifa district while bypassing the congestion of central Downtown.

Does Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai have the world's highest pool? Yes. The development features the highest outdoor infinity pool in the world, reserved exclusively for residents of the Trump Residences. The rooftop pool overlooks the full Dubai skyline and is one of the project's defining headline amenities.

Yes. The development features the highest outdoor infinity pool in the world, reserved exclusively for residents of the Trump Residences. The rooftop pool overlooks the full Dubai skyline and is one of the project's defining headline amenities.

What is the Trump Members-Only Club? The Trump Members-Only Club is a private club within the tower, accessible by annual membership fee with residents receiving priority access. It includes a signature bar, cigar lounge, golf simulator, spa treatment rooms, meeting rooms, and a dedicated pool.

The Trump Members-Only Club is a private club within the tower, accessible by annual membership fee with residents receiving priority access. It includes a signature bar, cigar lounge, golf simulator, spa treatment rooms, meeting rooms, and a dedicated pool.

What amenities are exclusive to residents of the Trump Residences? Residents have exclusive access to the world's highest rooftop infinity pool, a state-of-the-art gym, an owners lounge, and a children's play area. Membership to the Trump Members-Only Club is available separately for access to the wider lifestyle facilities within the building.
