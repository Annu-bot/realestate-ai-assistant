Can foreign nationals buy Trump Golf Villas in Oman? Yes. Oman offers 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities on qualifying Integrated Tourism Complex (ITC) properties. Buyers in ITC-registered projects are also eligible for a lifetime residency visa. Buyers should confirm specific eligibility conditions with the DarGlobal sales team or their legal advisor.

What are the tax advantages of buying property in Oman? Oman applies 0% personal income tax and 0% inheritance tax, with inheritance assessed under the investor's own country's laws rather than local law. Businesses operating in special economic and free zones benefit from a 25-year exemption from corporate income taxes. The Omani Rial is pegged to the US Dollar, providing currency stability for international investors.
