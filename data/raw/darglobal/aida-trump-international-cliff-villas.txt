What are Trump Cliff Villas at AIDA? Trump Cliff Villas are a collection of fully furnished three-bedroom sea-view villas at AIDA, Muscat, developed by DarGlobal. Each villa is positioned directly beside the Trump International Hotel at 130 metres above the Muscat Riviera, with a private pool overlooking the sea. Villas are designed and furnished to the highest standard, with every detail specified down to specification.

What are the villa sizes and unit types available? Trump Cliff Villas offer three-bedroom furnished residences from 128 to 166 sqm. Each villa includes a private pool with sea views. Owners receive a Trump International Golf Club Social Membership as part of the purchase and have the option to reside in or lease the villa.

What does the Trump Cliff Villas offer that other AIDA products do not? Trump Cliff Villas offer full villa ownership with a private pool, the closest positioning to the Trump International Hotel, and the most elevated sea views on the AIDA estate. Unlike Trump Golf Villas, which face the golf course, These villas are positioned on the cliff edge with direct sea orientation. Both villa products sit within the same AIDA estate and share access to the same championship golf course and community facilities.

Can foreign nationals buy Trump Cliff Villas in Oman? Yes. Oman offers 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities on qualifying Integrated Tourism Complex (ITC) properties. ITC project buyers are eligible for a lifetime residency visa in Oman. An exclusive mortgage program is available in Oman for both resident and non-resident buyers. Buyers should confirm specific eligibility conditions with DarGlobal or their legal advisor.

What are the tax advantages of investing in Oman real estate? Oman applies 0% personal income tax, 0% capital gains tax, and 0% inheritance tax, with inheritance assessed under the investor's own country's laws. Businesses in special economic and free zones benefit from a 25-year exemption from corporate income taxes.
